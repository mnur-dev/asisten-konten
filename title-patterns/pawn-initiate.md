# Pawn Initiate — title patterns

Measured 2026-09-22 on the channel's 1,353 public videos (`pawn-initiate.json`, same
folder): 753 Shorts (≤180 s, median 40,303 views) and 600 long videos (>180 s, median
1,225 views). "Top" = the best 10% by views, "bottom" = the worse half.

## Long videos

What separates the top from the bottom:

| Feature | Top 10% | Bottom 50% |
|---|---|---|
| "X vs Y" in the title | 18% | 37% |
| Contains "!" | 12% | 38% |
| Starts with "When …" | 18% | 16% |
| Contains a number (rating, age, rank) | 37% | 27% |
| ALL-CAPS word | 13% | 17% |
| Median length | 52 chars / 9 words | 50 chars / 9 words |

- **Lead with a story, not a pairing.** A title that is only "Player A vs Player B" is
  the single most over-represented pattern among the weak videos. When a pairing is
  needed, it goes after a `|` at the end: "Chess Veteran try to beat Magnus Carlsen |
  Ivanchuk vs Magnus" (76k).
- **"When [player] [does something]"** is the channel's strongest long-video opener:
  "When Praggnanandhaa Checkmates Magnus Carlsen" (127k), "When Praggnanandhaa Slowly
  Shuts Down Hikaru" (99k), "When Magnus can't Understand Fabiano Caruana" (72k).
- **A character or a gap between players**, made concrete with a number: "The Man who
  STUNNED Hikaru Nakamura" (74k), "15 Year-old Gukesh Destroys Baadur Jobava" (60k),
  "Magnus Tricks a 2300 Rated Player and Checkmates Him" (54k), "Vietnam's No. 1 chess
  player challenges Magnus Carlsen" (314k).
- **A strong verb carries the drama** — Destroys, Crushed, Stunned, Shuts Down,
  Checkmates, Sacrifices the Queen — rather than exclamation marks.
- **A twist or aftermath**: "He Tried the Magnus Gambit Against Magnus. Then This
  Happened" (458k), "Javokhir Sindarov Defeats Kramnik, Who Walks Off Immediately"
  (84k), "Magnus looks Tired after playing Javokhir Sindarov" (503k).
- **Stand on its own.** The channel's suggested-traffic share is ~2%; titles that only
  make sense with tournament context don't travel. Put the tournament name last, if at all.
- No hashtags and almost no emoji in long titles.

## Shorts

- Short and emotional: median 7 words, ~45 characters.
- A big name up front — Magnus appears in 52% of top Shorts vs 38% of the bottom half;
  Hikaru, Pragg, Hans, Nepo, Sindarov, Gukesh follow.
- A reaction or a single moment: "He Missed Something!" (1.2M), "Hans Niemann Mocked
  Ian's Opening Choice!!" (900k), "Magnus in time trouble, what can he do ??!" (663k),
  "Pragg couldn't believe Magnus brilliancy to end this game..." (555k).
- Rank framing works: "roller coaster ending Germany no. 1 vs World No. 1...." (2.4M),
  "Game between World no. 1 & World no. 2 is always interensting...." (802k).
- "!", "...", 1–2 emoji (🤯 😱) and trailing hashtags (#chess #magnuscarlsen) are normal
  here and appear equally in top and bottom — use them lightly, never instead of content.

## Rules for every suggestion

- English, natural, no clickbait lies: every claim must be true of this game (who won,
  a real sacrifice, a real blunder, a real rating gap, time trouble only if the clock
  shows it).
- Never copy the source video's title; rework its angle.
- Don't duplicate a recent Pawn Initiate title, and don't give a long video the same
  title as a Short of the same game (twin titles on the same day lost badly: the Short
  "The Most Intense Game Between Magnus and Hans Niemann" got 27,467 views, the long
  version with the same title 137).
- Long videos ≤ 70 characters; Shorts ≤ 60. YouTube's hard limit is 100.
